An FAQ chatbot that answers questions and captures leads

We add a chatbot to your website for UK businesses. It answers common questions and captures a name and contact details. It does not make medical or health claims, it does not book by voice or phone, and WhatsApp stays a plain contact button with no AI.

What problem it solves

Many businesses lose clients because they don’t respond in time, repeat the same questions manually, or leave conversations without follow-up. A generic chatbot doesn’t help either if it sounds robotic or doesn’t understand your business.

  • After-hours enquiries that never get answered
  • Teams overwhelmed answering the same questions again and again
  • Unqualified leads that consume sales time
  • Enquiries that leave without a name or contact detail

How we do it

We write the answers from your real FAQs and connect the chatbot to a short form for name and contact details. Booking, if you want it, is a Cal.com calendar on the site — not something the chatbot does by phone.

  • A list of common questions and approved answers
  • Capture of name and contact details
  • Placed on the website only
  • No voice booking and no WhatsApp assistant

Example use case

A service business with repeated questions: the chatbot answers opening hours, what is included, and how to get in touch. If the visitor wants to continue, it asks for a name and contact details. It does not give medical advice and it does not book a call by voice.
