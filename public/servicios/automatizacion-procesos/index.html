Forms, calendar booking, and an FAQ chatbot

For UK businesses we add lead capture forms, Cal.com booking, and a website chatbot that answers common questions and stores a name and contact details. WhatsApp is only a contact button. There is no AI WhatsApp assistant and no voice booking.

What problem it solves

When a visitor cannot leave their details or pick a time, the enquiry ends on the page. A form and a calendar give them a clear next step without a phone agent or a WhatsApp bot.

  • Leads that arrive via form but nobody follows up
  • Duplicate or incomplete data across tools
  • Sales teams wasting time on repetitive tasks
  • No visibility into where each opportunity stands

How we do it

We add the form, connect Cal.com for booking, and place an FAQ chatbot on the website. WhatsApp stays a contact button. There is no automated follow-up sequence and no CRM integration in this offer.

  • Lead flow audit and leakage points
  • Lead capture forms on the website
  • Calendar booking with Cal.com, not a voice agent
  • Team alerts when a lead needs human attention

Example use case

A service business taking enquiries online: the page has a short form for name and contact details, a Cal.com calendar for a strategy call, and a chatbot that answers opening hours and what is included. WhatsApp is a button that opens a chat with the business. Nothing is booked by voice.
