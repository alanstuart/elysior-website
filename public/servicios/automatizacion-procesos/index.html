Services · Automation

Process automation that doesn’t leave leads behind

We connect forms, CRM, email, WhatsApp, and tools you already use so every lead receives its next step automatically — confirmations, follow-up, assignment to the right team member, or appointment booking — without relying on spreadsheets or human memory.

What problem it solves

When a visitor fills out a form and nobody responds in time, or the data gets stuck in an email without follow-up, money is lost. Manual automation (copy-pasting between apps) doesn’t scale either and creates errors that damage the customer experience.

  • Leads that arrive via form but nobody follows up
  • Duplicate or incomplete data across tools
  • Sales teams wasting time on repetitive tasks
  • No visibility into where each opportunity stands

How we do it

We map your current flow from first contact to sale or appointment. We design automations with clear rules, integrate the tools you already have (or recommend the right ones), and test every scenario before going live.

  • Lead flow audit and leakage points
  • Integration: forms → CRM → email → WhatsApp → calendar
  • Assignment, tagging, and automatic follow-up rules
  • Team alerts when a lead needs human attention

Example use case

Restaurant with reservations and private events: every web form enquiry triggers an automatic sequence: confirmation email to the client, notification to the events manager, CRM entry tagged by request type (table, event, catering), and a 24-hour reminder if there was no human response. The team stopped losing weekend requests and cut response time from hours to minutes.
