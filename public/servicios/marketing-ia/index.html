Google Ads landing pages and initial campaign setup

For UK businesses we build the landing page and can complete the initial Google Ads campaign setup. We do not manage campaigns month to month. Ad spend is paid by you to the ad platform.

What problem it solves

Paid clicks are wasted when they land on a page that does not match the offer. A landing page written for one service gives the visitor a clear next step: a form, a calendar booking, or a WhatsApp contact button.

  • Campaigns with clicks but few real enquiries
  • Content that doesn’t connect with the value proposition
  • Unqualified leads that overwhelm the sales team
  • No measurement between ads, landing pages, and follow-up

How we do it

We write the landing page around one offer and, on the Elite package, complete the initial Google Ads campaign setup. We do not manage the campaign after that. Ad spend is paid by you to the ad platform.

  • Audience, messaging, and conversion point diagnosis
  • A landing page written for one offer
  • Initial campaign setup only, not ongoing management
  • Ad spend stays with you and the ad platform

Example use case

A service business running ads: the landing page states one offer, a short form, and a Cal.com booking link. If the Elite package is included, we complete the initial campaign setup. After launch, the client manages the campaign and pays the ad platform directly.
